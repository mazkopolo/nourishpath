What NourishPath does

Useful guidance without the clutter

Scan, understand, log and review. NourishPath brings the important pieces together in one calm, approachable experience.

★

Explain food ratings

Estimate an AU/NZ Health Star Rating and see which nutrients—such as saturated fat, sugars and sodium—are driving it.

⌁

Track your day

Log food and drink portions, compare intake with educational daily targets and review trends over time.

✓

Respect your preferences

Consider selected dietary patterns, allergies and health cautions when reviewing foods and suggesting meal ideas.

◎

Scan labels and recipes

Use the camera to read a nutrition panel or recipe, then verify uncertain values before calculating a result.

＋

Search AU & NZ foods

Explore food-composition records from Australia and New Zealand, including useful vitamins and minerals.

⌂

Private by design

No NourishPath account, advertising or analytics. Profiles and food logs are stored locally on your device.

Important

Educational guidance—not medical advice

NourishPath is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prevent, monitor or treat disease. Ratings, targets and suggestions are educational estimates. Discuss nutrition targets, dietary changes and health decisions with your GP, doctor, registered dietitian, nutritionist or another appropriately qualified professional.

Food composition can vary by brand, formulation, preparation and serving size. Always check the manufacturer’s current label and ingredients, particularly when managing an allergy or medical condition.
