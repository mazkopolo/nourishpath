Privacy policy

Your information stays with you.

NourishPath is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics or a developer-operated server.

1. Privacy summary

NourishPath does not collect your profile or food-log information. Information you enter is processed and stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account service, advertising network, analytics service or cloud database for the app.

2. Information you provide

You may choose to enter information such as:

  • Name or initials and an optional profile photograph.
  • Year of birth, biological sex, height, weight and activity level.
  • Selected dietary preferences, allergies and health conditions.
  • Foods, drinks, portions, recipes and daily intake records.
  • Manual corrections to scanned nutrition information and personal target adjustments.

This information is used on your device to provide app features such as educational nutrition targets, food guidance, intake tracking and meal suggestions.

3. How information is stored

Body profiles, preferences, target settings and daily food logs are stored locally within NourishPath’s application storage on your device. NourishPath does not intentionally upload this information to a server operated by the developer.

Optional Face ID or device authentication can be used to protect changes to profile information and nutrition targets. Authentication is performed by Apple’s operating system; NourishPath does not receive or store your biometric data.

4. Optional device permissions

Camera

Used when you choose to scan a nutrition label or recipe, or take a profile photograph. Text recognition is performed on the device using Apple Vision. NourishPath does not upload captured images to a developer server.

Photo library

Used only when you choose a photograph for a body profile.

Microphone and speech recognition

Used only when you choose to speak a food and quantity.

Notifications

Used for reminders you enable, including daily intake, weekly review and periodic profile-update reminders. You can change these permissions in device Settings.

5. Apple Speech recognition

Voice food entry uses Apple’s Speech framework. Depending on your language, device and Apple settings, Apple may process audio or speech data to provide a transcription. This processing is governed by Apple’s terms and privacy practices. Voice entry is optional; typed search can be used instead.

6. Data collection, sale and sharing

NourishPath does not sell your personal information. The current app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send body profiles or food logs to the developer.

Apple may process information necessary to provide App Store distribution, notifications, speech recognition, operating-system diagnostics or other Apple services. Those activities are controlled by Apple and your Apple device settings.

7. Retention, correction and deletion

Information remains on the device until you edit or delete it, remove a profile or food-log entry, or delete the app. Deleting NourishPath removes its locally stored application data, subject to Apple device backups and operating-system behaviour.

Because the developer does not receive this local information, we cannot retrieve, correct or delete it remotely.

8. Children

NourishPath is not directed to children and is not presented as a Kids Category app. A parent or guardian should supervise any use by a minor. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

9. Nutrition data and external sources

The app includes food-composition information from Australian and New Zealand sources and may link to source websites for attribution or further information. External websites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not govern them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when NourishPath’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the app or its App Store information where appropriate.
