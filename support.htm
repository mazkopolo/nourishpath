Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

The camera or label scanner is not working

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and enable NourishPath. Use bright, even lighting, hold the phone parallel to the panel and keep the full nutrition table in focus. Review every extracted value before relying on the rating.

The app read a nutrition value incorrectly

Open “Review label values” and correct the number manually. Nutrition layouts vary considerably, so the app flags uncertain or missing values for verification. Always compare scanned values with the current package.

Voice food entry is unavailable

Open Settings → Privacy & Security and allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for NourishPath. Voice entry is optional; you can always type a food name in search.

I cannot choose a profile photograph

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → NourishPath and allow access to selected photos or the full library. You can also use the in-app camera option.

Notifications are not arriving

Open Settings → Notifications → NourishPath and enable Allow Notifications. Inside NourishPath, confirm that the specific reminder has been enabled. Focus modes and Scheduled Summary may delay delivery.

How do I remove my information?

Delete individual food logs or body profiles in the app. Deleting NourishPath removes its locally stored data from the device, subject to Apple device backups and operating-system behaviour. NourishPath has no developer account or remote profile to delete.

Is the Health Star Rating official?

NourishPath provides an estimate using available label or composition data. A result may differ from a manufacturer’s official rating when information such as ingredient proportions, category, density or formulation is unavailable. Database foods and scanned recipes are particularly likely to be estimates.

Can NourishPath manage a health condition?

No. Profile cautions, daily targets and meal suggestions are educational only. They are not diagnosis, treatment or an individual medical nutrition plan. Consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Safety reminder

Do not use NourishPath for emergencies, diagnosis or treatment. Always check package ingredients when managing an allergy. Discuss significant dietary changes, nutrition targets and health-condition decisions with your GP, doctor, registered dietitian, nutritionist or another appropriately qualified professional.
